## Purpose

From site-vision.md §5 and §7: often the first page someone sees, because articles get forwarded (by
John Schrimpf, or with a resume follow-up to Amy Schrimpf). It has to stand on its own: someone arriving
cold should learn who wrote it and how to reach you without going to Home. Every article ends with the
same footer: one line on who you are, one on what you're building toward, and a contact link.

### Questions

1. What appears under the title: date only, date and reading time, or date and "updated" date?
2. Should the article footer include the proof figures, or just the two sentences and a contact link?
3. Should related articles or "next article" appear at the end? With only a few articles, this probably
   isn't worth it yet.
4. Do articles need images or diagrams? The vision allows "abstract diagrams drawn for a specific
   article". That affects whether we need a figure/caption component.
5. Code blocks: will any articles include code? If not, we don't need to style them yet.
