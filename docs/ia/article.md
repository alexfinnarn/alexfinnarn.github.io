# Article

- **URL:** `/writing/<slug>`
- **Status:** draft
- **Type:** template (one layout used by every article)

## Purpose

Often the first page someone sees, because articles get forwarded. It has to stand on its own:
someone arriving cold should learn who wrote it and have somewhere to go next without visiting Home.

## Decisions

- Under the title: published date, updated date (when edited after publishing), and reading time.
- No related or "next" articles for now.
- Each article ends with its own CTA card that flows from the article's content. It isn't the same
  on every article. CTAs may be written with an AI agent and A/B tested.
- Figures (image plus caption) when an article calls for one.
- Code blocks use default rendering for now; no syntax highlighting until articles actually use code.
