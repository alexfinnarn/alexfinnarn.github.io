## Purpose

From site-vision.md §5 and §9: the shortest path to reaching you. It lists your email (the same
protonmail address as the resume), LinkedIn, and location, with one sentence on what to write to you
about and a response-time commitment you can keep.

### Questions

1. Email link only, or also a contact form? A form needs a third-party service on a static site (Formspree
   or similar), which adds a dependency and a privacy question. A plain `mailto:` link is simplest.
2. Should the email address be written out in full on the page (easy to copy, visible to scrapers) or
   only as a link?
3. Is a phone number appropriate for this audience? Manufacturing owners often prefer a call.
4. Should there be a link to book a call (Calendly or similar)?
5. GitHub: does it belong on Contact for this audience, or only in the footer?
