# Contact

- **URL:** `/contact`

## Purpose

How to reach Alex, and a chance to book time with him.

## Decisions

- No plain email link, and the address isn't shown on the page. The visitor does something before the contact form appears. (The old site had a matching game of Alex's principles; nothing that elaborate is needed to start.)
- No phone number.
- A way to book a call. Google Calendar's appointment schedules (a booking page tied to Alex's free/busy time) may do this without Calendly. Check what Alex's account includes.
- LinkedIn stays in the footer as a way in that doesn't need the gate. GitHub and Medium are there too, each with its logo (2026-11-14).
- Until the gate and form are built, the page lists LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium and location, with a Fill in slot for the form. The booking link is hidden until Alex sets it up (2026-11-14).
- The positioning paragraph that used to be Home's lede sits at the top of this page (2026-11-14), since the forest scene on Home has no place for it. The colored-pencil portrait from About sits beside it.
- Form delivery: Formspree when the form is built **(default)**. It works from a static site, has a free tier, and keeps the address off the page.
